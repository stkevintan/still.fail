# Moving to another station (for agents)

A migration between two workspace stations is independent modules ordered by a scheduler (左子健, 2026-11-14: 「每个模块独立迁移，有依赖的模块显式给出依赖，要求先迁依赖项，模块内部带进度迁移，可回滚」). Both stations keep working; a whole station is every item, connects and logins last; moving back is the way back. It replaces `station/src/replica/retire.ts` (first real use, 2026-11-14: chats paused 18 minutes, half moved, no way back), whose start stays off.

## The seam and the scheduler

- `module.ts`: a module plans items (needs only of modules it declares; why one cannot move, in words saying what to do), moves one, moves one back, says where each stands; item state is durable, so a restarted side goes on or undoes. `scheduler.ts` only orders by needs (unmet: refused, named; `withNeeds` moves needs first, but not one whose leaving would stop items here; a need `usableThere` is neither awaited nor taken), moves back in reverse (refused while something there needs it), tells changes. No polling.
- An item moving away first stops every item here that needs it and can no longer have it (`stopped.log`, held again at start), never left to fail (左子健, 2026-11-14: 「依赖项被迁走就停掉啊」). "Needs" is "usable on this station" (`usableHere`: an account this station only borrows stops nothing). A stopped chat is held like one handed over (no turn, jobs kept stopped, input delivered once where it ends up, nothing posted, agent not woken) until its need returns or it moves. The plan says what a move would stop (`wouldStop`, the turn's own predicate: scheduler `#stoppable`, only modules with `stop`; a routed connect goes on, threads routed).
- An item may go either way to where a need is not usable: it arrives stopped and goes on when that arrives (the owner, both ways, 2026-11-14). The arrival station keeps it in its own stopped set (`stoppedFor`, scheduler `noteStopped`), told when the need comes (`move.arrived`). Forward goes needs-first unless `arriveStopped`; back, what needs an item there goes first, arriving stopped, then the item. Modules may need each other (chats and connects do); a cycle of items is refused, named.

## The modules

Each module file's head says what its item is and how it moves. Beyond that:

- `accounts` (needs none; `accounts.ts`): this machine's own login never goes. A chat needs only the login it runs on now; ones it only ran on or lists are no need (handover.ts `pastLeft`; the owner, 2026-11-16: 「只是以前用过、又迁不走的登录不再卡住聊天」): it says what it `leaves` and arrives with them missing (`nowhere`), their state staying here for its return. A shareable login (`shareRefusal`) needed only by what moves is lent, not moved (the owner, 2026-11-15: its host never leaves, so nothing strands on a station that dies or leaves): shared with the target before dependents go (share/index.ts `lendTo`, `share.borrow`), kept lent (`accounts-lent.log`, scheduler `lentFor`), taken back by this station alone once the last item it was lent for is here again and the other says nothing there runs on it (`move.uses`, asked each turn's end; drill 2026-11-15 took it back from under five), or when the borrower reports its last chat on it gone (`move.unused`, `lookAt`); plan and page show it (`lentTo`, `lent`). Asked by name or in a whole move, its host moves (`moveProfile`) after what needs it; one that cannot be shared (SuperGrok, Cursor, Devin, a Kimi sign-in) moves as is, dependents stopped first. One borrowed from a third station is neither's (real run, 2026-11-16: a whole move failed on two such logins): no item where the other borrows it too; one the other lacks cannot go, dependents refused or arriving stopped until the third shares it (move/station.ts `loginsChanged`).
- `chats` (needs accounts, connects, repos, vault; `chats.ts`): one chat by `station/src/replica/handover.ts`, files mirrored by difference within its room.
- `jobs` (needs chats; `jobs.ts`): never on two stations: they move right after their chat, back before it; one back while its chat is there waits stopped.
- `connects` (needs chats; `connects.ts`): a Comma or single-session connect goes before its chats and keeps what its platform says, in order, until all arrived (`connect.take` `awaiting`, connects/workspace.ts `arrived`), never a new session for one on its way. From the ask on, this station's socket hands events to the holder (`handing`: sockets overlap a few ms; Slack gives an event to any of an app's sockets). The move is kept on disk (`connects-moving`) until an answer is heard; both ask each other at start and when heard (`settleMoving`, `connect.holds`): exactly one holds it.
- `repos`, `vault`, `settings` (`repos.ts`, `vault.ts`, `settings.ts`) copy, so moving one stops nothing (`usableHere`); back removes only what the move made. Settings never overwrite the other's (the owner: 「不好搬但有其它办法规避的部分直接规避，避免脚本乱改改错」). What moved is kept across a start (`kept.ts`). Grants do not move: the owner approves again.
- Agents' memory, team skills, presets and starred models are no module: already the same everywhere (`station/src/share/AGENTS.md`; the lead, 2026-11-14: what need not move is not). memory.ts machine-local files stay.

## The page, plans and requests

- `GET /move?to=` (`routes.ts`) is told changed as `move` (bursts once per 500 ms, live.ts). While a turn toward that station runs, the page comes from the turn's plan (scheduler `held`, `current`): it pages no chats and asks nothing of the other (`station/AGENTS.md`: index and limit). station_move plan and status answer a summary, items a page or one at a time (tools/move.ts `summaryOf`, `pageOf`), never all (one answer is one line of mesh/peer.ts `MAX_MESSAGE`; stations hold thousands of chats).
- A plan never guesses about the other: an unanswered question (`move.accounts`, `move.chats.live`, `move.repo.have`, `move.setting.values`) makes what it asks about unable to go this turn, said and logged. A station found gone in a turn is asked nothing more; later steps fail at once in its words. A connection the other's process closed while stopping or handing over (mesh/peer.ts `PeerStopped`, reason `STOPPING`) is not that: the request is asked once more (drill 7h). So every move handler takes a repeat as the same (ids, versions, what is there; rows by `page`; chat-handover-moves.test.ts). Words to people come from `station.move.*`, never ids.
- No request waits on work growing with a chat or repository (real run, 2026-11-16: a 70k-file chat placed in 50 s, past ANSWER_MS, never moved): it is a told step (`told.ts`: placing, pruning, `move.back`, `move.repo.bundle`, `move.repo.drop`, `move.tool`; `move.repo.made`). A stopped turn lets go at once (handover.ts `abandon`, told.ts `letGo`). `chat-handover-steps.test.ts` holds every move request to its time with work held ten minutes and fails on an unknown one: add a new request to its table with its bound.
- `POST /move`, `POST /move/back`: owners and admins, 202, one turn at a time for all (else 409), no card: the press confirms. A chat's item may be named by any of its sessions (`chatOf`): a chat's menu in the app names its own.
- The overview's `move.chats` lists the chats handing over from here now (handover.ts `moving`, the viewer's workspace's alone), from the first pass to gone or failed: a device shuts that chat's composer saying where it goes (core `ChatView.moving`), so a person never types into a chat about to pause.
- Moving is on for every station, stable and test channel, since it graduated (2026-11-21; drills: e2e-local.sh move-all); the overview says it as `move.offered`, which the app goes by, and still lists `stationMove` in `features` for apps from before (`features.ts` `GRADUATED_SAID`). Where a station's `moveOn` says not (a test's), `station_move` and stillfail-move are not offered, every turn is refused before anything is held (scheduler `refusal`, then `move.open` there), no `handover.begin` is taken, `GET /move` still answers. Turns and every `move.*`/`handover.*` request wait until the previous station process is gone (replica/handover.ts `previous`), refused in words past `PREVIOUS_LIMIT_MS` (ops/previous.ts, `station.move.previousStill`).
- A device's idempotent message (api/admin.ts `once`) goes with its chat (`handover.show`, `keptSends`, `keepSends`): a retry there is not posted again.

## Sizes, room and holds

- A chat's files go twice (`station/src/replica/handover.ts`): while it works, then held only for changes, the target keeping the first pass (`placed.txt`), so the pause does not grow with the room. It goes only where what that station lacks (sending.ts `adds`) leaves the floor free (handover.ts `floorOf`: the larger of 20 GiB and a tenth of the disk), not the whole (real run, 2026-11-15: 110 chats to Office-Host-2, free bytes did not fall); refused in numbers before it is held.
- Sizes are known before a move (`sizes.ts`, carry.mbt `carry_sizes`, the gathering's own walk), measured again only for changed stamps (chats.ts `stampOf`) and never while a turn runs here. A whole move waits for them, asks the other (`move.sizes`, `move.open`) and is refused before holding anything where the floor would not stay free (`roomForAll`); unanswered, each chat is checked as it goes.
- A turn holds the station's self-update from first item to end; a station taking a request or chat holds it meanwhile (`station/src/updates/holds.ts`). A stopped turn is `last` (`turn.json`); at start, and when a station is heard again, waiters whose item is here go on (`settleStopped`). `POST /move/stop` and station_move `stop`: nothing further starts, a chat not yet gone stays, one gone completes.
- `station_move` (`station/src/tools/move.ts`, `station/assets/skills/stillfail-move.md`) is the scheduler's face: one chat or item needs no card (seconds of pause, undone by moving back); `all` (paging through every chat) is confirmed once on an owner or admin card in the app (`MoveAsks`, choice `move`).
